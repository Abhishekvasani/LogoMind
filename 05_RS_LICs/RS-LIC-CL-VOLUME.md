# Color Intelligence — Starter Set (10 Colours)

> *Colour is the fastest path to emotion. Before the audience reads a word or parses a form, they feel the colour.*

Each entry covers the colour's emotional associations, industry conventions, cultural variations, accessibility considerations, and combination potential.

---

## RS-LIC-CL-001 — Blue

**Psychology:** Trust, stability, calm, professionalism, intelligence
**Industry Conventions:** Finance, technology, healthcare, corporate — the "safe" choice
**Variations:** Navy (authority, heritage), Royal (premium, confidence), Sky (optimism, openness), Teal-Blue (modern, fresh)
**Originality Risk:** 🔴 Very High — the most used colour in branding; needs distinctive treatment or pairing to differentiate
**Cultural Notes:** Near-universal positive; mourning in some Iranian contexts
**Best Pairings:** Orange (complementary energy), White (clarity), Yellow (optimism contrast)
**Accessibility:** Generally high contrast; test navy on dark backgrounds
**Best For:** Brands needing trust and stability — but must differentiate from the sea of blue competitors
**LKG Relationships:** → Trust, → Stability, → Professionalism

---

## RS-LIC-CL-002 — Green

**Psychology:** Growth, nature, health, renewal, sustainability, money (US)
**Industry Conventions:** Eco/sustainability, health, agriculture, finance (US)
**Variations:** Forest (organic, grounded), Lime (energy, fresh), Sage (calm, sophisticated), Emerald (luxury, precious)
**Originality Risk:** 🔴 High in eco/health (the default); 🟢 Lower in unexpected categories
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive; associated with Islam; luck in some cultures
**Best Pairings:** Brown (earthiness), White (freshness), Gold (premium-natural)
**Accessibility:** Medium contrast; test for colour-blindness (deuteranopia)
**Best For:** Sustainability, health, growth brands — but challenge the default green
**LKG Relationships:** → Growth, → Nature, → Health

---

## RS-LIC-CL-003 — Red

**Psychology:** Energy, passion, urgency, excitement, danger, appetite
**Industry Conventions:** Food (appetite stimulation), retail (urgency/sale), entertainment, sports
**Variations:** Crimson (premium, passionate), Coral (warm, approachable), Burgundy (luxury, heritage), Bright Red (energy, alarm)
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — powerful but common; impact depends on application
**Cultural Notes:** Luck/celebration (China), danger/warning (Western), mourning (South Africa)
**Best Pairings:** White (clarity, contrast), Black (drama, premium), Cream (warmth)
**Accessibility:** Low contrast on dark backgrounds; caution with green (colour-blindness)
**Best For:** Brands needing energy, appetite appeal, or urgency
**LKG Relationships:** → Energy, → Passion, → Urgency

---

## RS-LIC-CL-004 — Yellow / Gold

**Psychology:** Optimism, warmth, happiness, caution, premium (gold)
**Industry Conventions:** Children/education, budget/value, luxury (gold)
**Variations:** Lemon (energy, freshness), Mustard (sophisticated, warm), Gold (luxury, precious), Amber (warmth, comfort)
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — distinctive when used boldly; can disappear on white
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive (sunshine); mourning in some Latin American contexts; imperial in China
**Best Pairings:** Navy (premium contrast), Black (drama), Grey (sophistication)
**Accessibility:** Poor contrast on white; needs dark background or outline
**Best For:** Optimism, warmth, or premium (gold variant) brands
**LKG Relationships:** → Optimism, → Warmth, → Luxury (gold)

---

## RS-LIC-CL-005 — Purple

**Psychology:** Luxury, creativity, spirituality, mystery, royalty
**Industry Conventions:** Beauty, luxury, creative, spiritual/wellness
**Variations:** Deep Purple (luxury, mystery), Lavender (calm, gentle), Plum (sophisticated, rich), Violet (creative, spiritual)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low-Moderate — less common than blue/green; distinctive in most categories
**Cultural Notes:** Royalty/luxury (Western); mourning (some Latin American, Brazilian)
**Best Pairings:** Gold (royal luxury), Grey (modern sophistication), Cream (approachable elegance)
**Accessibility:** Generally good contrast
**Best For:** Creative, luxury, or spiritual brands wanting to differentiate from blue/green
**LKG Relationships:** → Creativity, → Luxury, → Spirituality

---

## RS-LIC-CL-006 — Orange

**Psychology:** Energy, enthusiasm, friendliness, affordability, creativity
**Industry Conventions:** Budget/value retail, creative, food, sports
**Variations:** Burnt Orange (warmth, earthiness), Bright Orange (energy, attention), Peach (soft, approachable), Terracotta (grounded, craft)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — less common than primary colours; distinctive and energetic
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive; safety/warning in some contexts; Buddhist tradition
**Best Pairings:** Navy (complementary), Teal (modern, fresh), Brown (earthy)
**Accessibility:** Medium contrast; test on white backgrounds
**Best For:** Approachable, energetic brands wanting to differentiate from red/yellow
**LKG Relationships:** → Energy, → Friendliness, → Creativity

---

## RS-LIC-CL-007 — Black

**Psychology:** Sophistication, authority, luxury, mystery, power, premium
**Industry Conventions:** Luxury, fashion, technology, editorial
**Variations:** Pure Black (maximum contrast, drama), Off-Black/Charcoal (softer, sophisticated), Matte Black (modern, premium)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — timeless; impact through application and restraint
**Cultural Notes:** Mourning (Western); can be associated with rebellion/counter-culture
**Best Pairings:** Gold (luxury), White (clarity), Any accent colour (black is the ultimate neutral)
**Accessibility:** Highest possible contrast (with white)
**Best For:** Premium, sophisticated, or minimalist brands
**LKG Relationships:** → Luxury, → Authority, → Sophistication

---

## RS-LIC-CL-008 — White / Cream

**Psychology:** Purity, cleanliness, simplicity, space, clarity, minimalism
**Industry Conventions:** Healthcare, minimalism, weddings, technology (Apple)
**Variations:** Pure White (clinical, stark), Cream (warm, organic), Ivory (luxury, soft), Off-White (approachable, less harsh)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — foundational; provides the space other colours need
**Cultural Notes:** Purity/weddings (Western); mourning (some Eastern cultures)
**Best Pairings:** Any — white is the canvas; lets other colours sing
**Accessibility:** Requires careful contrast management
**Best For:** Minimalist, healthcare, or space-creating brands
**LKG Relationships:** → Purity, → Clarity, → Minimalism

---

## RS-LIC-CL-009 — Brown / Earth Tones

**Psychology:** Earthiness, reliability, warmth, craft, tradition, organic
**Industry Conventions:** Craft, artisanal, outdoor, coffee/chocolate, heritage
**Variations:** Chocolate (rich, premium), Tan (approachable, warm), Mocha (sophisticated, warm), Rust (energetic earth)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — underused in modern branding; distinctive when applied boldly
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive; earthiness and groundedness associations
**Best Pairings:** Cream (warmth), Green (organic), Gold (premium-craft)
**Accessibility:** Good contrast with light colours
**Best For:** Craft, artisanal, or heritage brands wanting warmth and groundedness
**LKG Relationships:** → Craft, → Warmth, → Reliability

---

## RS-LIC-CL-010 — Teal / Turquoise

**Psychology:** Calm, freshness, sophistication, modern, tropical, clarity
**Industry Conventions:** Wellness, technology, travel/tropical, creative
**Variations:** Deep Teal (sophisticated, premium), Bright Turquoise (fresh, tropical), Mint (soft, approachable), Cyan (digital, technical)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — distinctive; stands out from the blue-green majority
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive; freshness and tropical associations
**Best Pairings:** Coral (tropical complementary), Navy (depth), Cream (warmth)
**Accessibility:** Generally good contrast
**Best For:** Wellness, modern tech, or travel brands wanting freshness without default blue
**LKG Relationships:** → Calm, → Freshness, → Sophistication

## Colour Selection Framework

| Brand Personality | Primary Recommendation | Avoid |
|-------------------|----------------------|-------|
| Trust / Stability | Navy Blue, Teal | Bright Red (conflict) |
| Energy / Passion | Red, Bright Orange | Pastels (too soft) |
| Growth / Nature | Green (non-default), Teal | Default leaf-green |
| Luxury / Premium | Black, Gold, Deep Purple | Bright primary colours |
| Health / Wellness | Teal, Green, White | Red (alarm), Black (clinical) |
| Creativity / Innovation | Purple, Orange, Teal | Grey (boring) |
| Craft / Artisanal | Brown, Cream, Forest Green | Neon colours |
| Technology / Modern | Geometric Blue, Black, Teal | Brown (dated) |
