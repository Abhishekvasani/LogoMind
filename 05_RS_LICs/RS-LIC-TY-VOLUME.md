# Typography Intelligence — Starter Set (10 Type Categories)

> *Typography is the voice of the brand. The letterform choice determines whether the brand whispers or shouts, embraces or distances, leads or follows.*

Each entry is a compact LIC covering the type category's personality, industry fit, emotional associations, pairing principles, and originality potential.

---

## RS-LIC-TY-001 — Humanist Sans-Serif

**Definition:** Sans-serif typefaces derived from calligraphic/handwritten forms; warm and approachable with varying stroke contrast.

**Personality:** Approachable, human, warm, credible, modern-but-not-cold
**Industry Fit:** Healthcare, education, service, nonprofit, lifestyle, B2C tech
**Emotional Associations:** Trust, friendliness, accessibility, care
**Examples:** Gill Sans, Frutiger, Optima, Myriad, Verdana
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — widely applicable; feels familiar without being generic
**Pairing:** Pairs well with serif body text for warmth + authority
**Best For:** Brands needing to feel human and approachable without losing professionalism
**LKG Relationships:** → Warmth, → Approachability, → Trust

---

## RS-LIC-TY-002 — Geometric Sans-Serif

**Definition:** Sans-serif typefaces built from clean geometric forms (circles, triangles, rectangles); precise and modern.

**Personality:** Precise, modern, efficient, technological, clean
**Industry Fit:** Technology, engineering, science, architecture, modern finance
**Emotional Associations:** Efficiency, intelligence, modernity, competence
**Examples:** Futura, Avenir, Circular, Gotham, Montserrat
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — very popular; can feel generic without customisation
**Pairing:** Pairs well with humanist sans for warmth or with mono for technical depth
**Best For:** Brands needing to feel precise and forward-looking
**LKG Relationships:** → Precision, → Modernity, → Efficiency

---

## RS-LIC-TY-003 — Grotesque / Neo-Grotesque Sans-Serif

**Definition:** Sans-serif typefaces with subtle stroke contrast and neutral personality; the workhorses of modern identity.

**Personality:** Neutral, objective, versatile, professional, timeless
**Industry Fit:** Universal — corporate, editorial, institutional, technology
**Emotional Associations:** Neutrality, reliability, clarity, authority
**Examples:** Helvetica, Univers, Akzidenz-Grotesk, Inter, Roboto
**Originality Risk:** 🔴 High (Helvetica/Inter) — extremely common; needs customisation or unusual application to stand out
**Pairing:** The most versatile; pairs with almost anything
**Best For:** Brands needing maximum versatility across many applications
**LKG Relationships:** → Clarity, → Neutrality, → Reliability

---

## RS-LIC-TY-004 — Transitional Serif

**Definition:** Serif typefaces with moderate contrast between thick and thin strokes; bridging old-style and modern.

**Personality:** Elegant, established, authoritative, refined, readable
**Industry Fit:** Publishing, law, finance, luxury, education, heritage
**Emotional Associations:** Authority, tradition, quality, sophistication
**Examples:** Times New Roman, Baskerville, Georgia, Miller
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — established but distinctive when customised
**Pairing:** Pairs well with geometric sans for tradition + modernity
**Best For:** Brands needing authority and heritage without feeling old-fashioned
**LKG Relationships:** → Authority, → Heritage, → Quality

---

## RS-LIC-TY-005 — Old-Style Serif

**Definition:** Serif typefaces with low contrast and diagonal stress; the oldest serif tradition.

**Personality:** Classic, scholarly, enduring, intellectual, timeless
**Industry Fit:** Education, law, publishing, religion, heritage institutions
**Emotional Associations:** Wisdom, permanence, scholarship, gravitas
**Examples:** Garamond, Caslon, Jenson, Sabon
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — classic but not overused in modern branding
**Pairing:** Pairs well with humanist sans for scholarly + approachable
**Best For:** Brands with genuine heritage or scholarly authority
**LKG Relationships:** → Wisdom, → Heritage, → Timelessness

---

## RS-LIC-TY-006 — Modern / Didone Serif

**Definition:** Serif typefaces with extreme contrast between thick/thin strokes and vertical stress; high fashion aesthetic.

**Personality:** Dramatic, elegant, luxurious, bold, fashion-forward
**Industry Fit:** Fashion, luxury, beauty, cosmetics, high-end hospitality
**Emotional Associations:** Luxury, sophistication, drama, exclusivity
**Examples:** Bodoni, Didot, Playfair Display
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — distinctive; high-impact even at small sizes when well-applied
**Pairing:** Pairs well with clean geometric sans for luxury + modernity
**Best For:** Premium brands needing visual drama and sophistication
**LKG Relationships:** → Luxury, → Elegance, → Exclusivity

---

## RS-LIC-TY-007 — Slab Serif

**Definition:** Serif typefaces with block-like, rectangular serifs; robust and confident.

**Personality:** Robust, confident, distinctive, industrial, editorial
**Industry Fit:** Editorial, technology, industrial, creative, food/beverage
**Emotional Associations:** Strength, character, approachability (some slabs), distinctiveness
**Examples:** Rockwell, Clarendon, Roboto Slab, Archer
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — less common than sans; stands out in most categories
**Pairing:** Pairs well with matching sans family for consistency
**Best For:** Brands needing character and robustness without stuffiness
**LKG Relationships:** → Strength, → Character, → Distinctiveness

---

## RS-LIC-TY-008 — Script / Calligraphic

**Definition:** Typefaces mimicking handwriting or formal calligraphy; personal and expressive.

**Personality:** Personal, elegant, artisanal, expressive, human
**Industry Fit:** Luxury, wedding/event, beauty, artisanal food, personal services
**Emotional Associations:** Elegance, craft, intimacy, tradition, artistry
**Examples:** Edwardian Script, Snell Roundhand, Brush Script, custom lettering
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — can feel dated if not custom; custom lettering is highly original
**Pairing:** Use for logos/headers only; pair with clean sans for body
**Best For:** Brands needing personal touch or artisanal credibility
**Caveat:** Legibility at small sizes is a major concern; test thoroughly
**LKG Relationships:** → Craft, → Elegance, → Intimacy

---

## RS-LIC-TY-009 — Monospace

**Definition:** Typefaces where every character occupies equal width; derived from typewriters and code.

**Personality:** Technical, precise, honest, code-native, functional
**Industry Fit:** Developer tools, cybersecurity, data/analytics, engineering, archival
**Emotional Associations:** Precision, transparency, technical credibility, nostalgia
**Examples:** Courier, IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono, Fira Code
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — distinctive in most categories; signals technical authenticity
**Pairing:** Pairs well with geometric sans for technical + clean
**Best For:** Technical brands needing to signal developer credibility
**LKG Relationships:** → Precision, → Technical, → Transparency

---

## RS-LIC-TY-010 — Display / Custom

**Definition:** Typefaces designed specifically for large-size display use; often bespoke for a single brand.

**Personality:** Distinctive, brand-specific, impossible to replicate, premium
**Industry Fit:** Any category where maximum differentiation is needed
**Emotional Associations:** Confidence, investment, uniqueness, craft
**Examples:** Coca-Cola (custom script), Google (custom geometric), Airbnb (custom Cereal), Netflix (custom)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Lowest — by definition unique
**Pairing:** Bespoke display type pairs with a versatile text family for body
**Best For:** Brands investing in long-term distinctiveness; the ultimate typography differentiation
**Caveat:** Expensive to commission; requires experienced type designer
**LKG Relationships:** → Originality, → Craft, → Distinctiveness

## Typography Selection Framework

When the Create Engine recommends typography direction, it applies this framework:

| Brand Personality | Primary Recommendation | Alternative |
|-------------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Warm / Approachable | Humanist Sans | Slab Serif |
| Precise / Modern | Geometric Sans | Monospace |
| Authoritative / Heritage | Transitional Serif | Old-Style Serif |
| Luxurious / Dramatic | Modern/Didone Serif | Script |
| Technical / Honest | Monospace | Geometric Sans |
| Distinctive / Bespoke | Custom Display | Slab Serif |
| Neutral / Versatile | Grotesque (customised) | Geometric Sans |
