# Symbol Intelligence — Starter Set (15 Symbols)

> *Symbols are the vocabulary of visual identity. This volume gives the Create Engine a structured library of the most frequently encountered symbols — with meanings, originality assessments, cultural considerations, and combination possibilities.*

Each symbol is a compact LIC: Definition, Meanings, Industries, Emotional Associations, Originality Risk, Combination Potential, and LKG Relationships.

---

## RS-LIC-SY-001 — Circle

**Definition:** A continuous curved form with no beginning, end, or corners.

**Primary Meanings:** Unity, wholeness, continuity, community, infinity
**Secondary Meanings:** Protection, cycle, completeness, harmony
**Industries:** Healthcare, community, technology, finance, education
**Emotional Associations:** Trust, calm, inclusiveness, safety
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — extremely common; needs strong internal concept to differentiate
**Combination Potential:** High — circles combine well with letters, negative space, and geometric divisions
**Cultural Notes:** Near-universal positive associations; minimal cultural risk
**Avoid When:** The category is saturated with circular marks (many tech and wellness brands)
**LKG Relationships:** → Unity (RS-LIC-PH-001 Meaning), → Trust, → Harmony

---

## RS-LIC-SY-002 — Triangle

**Definition:** A three-sided polygon; the simplest enclosed geometric form after the circle.

**Primary Meanings:** Direction, progress, stability, ascent, hierarchy
**Secondary Meanings:** Precision, warning, dynamic tension, balance
**Industries:** Technology, engineering, finance, energy, outdoor
**Emotional Associations:** Energy, ambition, focus, determination
**Originality Risk:** 🟠 Moderate — common in tech and finance; needs distinctive treatment
**Combination Potential:** High — triangles combine with letters, point patterns, and fractal structures
**Cultural Notes:** Generally positive; in some contexts can suggest danger/warning (inverted)
**LKG Relationships:** → Progress, → Precision, → Direction

---

## RS-LIC-SY-003 — Hexagon

**Definition:** A six-sided polygon; the most efficient tessellating shape in nature.

**Primary Meanings:** Precision, engineering, nature (honeycomb), efficiency, structure
**Secondary Meanings:** Collaboration (interlocking), science, modularity
**Industries:** Engineering, technology, chemistry, construction, sustainability
**Emotional Associations:** Competence, reliability, intelligence
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low-Moderate — less exhausted than circles/triangles; still distinctive in most categories
**Combination Potential:** Very high — tessellates, contains other shapes, integrates with letters
**Cultural Notes:** Minimal cultural risk; strong natural science associations
**LKG Relationships:** → Precision, → Efficiency, → Structure

---

## RS-LIC-SY-004 — Bridge

**Definition:** A structure spanning and connecting two points; a metaphor for connection.

**Primary Meanings:** Connection, transition, linking, overcoming division
**Secondary Meanings:** Trust, journey, support, partnership
**Industries:** Finance, consulting, technology, logistics, healthcare
**Emotional Associations:** Reliability, progress, hope, unity
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — metaphorical, not literal; less exhausted than geometric shapes
**Combination Potential:** High — bridges combine with water, horizons, letters, negative space

**LKG Relationships:** → Trust, → Connection, → Partnership

## RS-LIC-SY-011 — Leaf

**Definition:** The primary organ of photosynthesis; metaphor for nature, growth, and sustainability.

**Primary Meanings:** Nature, growth, sustainability, renewal
**Secondary Meanings:** Eco-friendly, organic, health, freshness
**Industries:** Sustainability, food, health, agriculture, beauty
**Emotional Associations:** Freshness, vitality, naturalness, health

**Industries:** Logistics, technology, delivery, finance, sports

## RS-LIC-SY-015 — Monogram / Letter Integration

**Definition:** A mark formed from one or more letters of the brand name; the most personalised symbol type.

**Primary Meanings:** Identity, ownership, personalisation, heritage
**Secondary Meanings:** Craft (custom-designed), authority (institutional monograms), brevity
**Industries:** Universal — works across all categories
**Emotional Associations:** Personal, established, distinctive (when well-crafted)
**Originality Risk:** 🟢 Low — inherently unique (each brand has different initials); execution determines quality
**Combination Potential:** Very high — monograms combine with geometric forms, symbols, negative space
**Cultural Notes:** Minimal risk; monograms are understood across cultures
**LKG Relationships:** → Identity, → Personalisation, → Heritage
