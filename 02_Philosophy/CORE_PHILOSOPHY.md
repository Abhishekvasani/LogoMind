# LogoMind Core Philosophy

> **"We are not building software. We are building a methodology that software delivers."**

---

## The North Star

> Transform the quality of design decisions.
> Knowledge becomes judgment. Judgment becomes better logos.

---

## Foundational Beliefs

1. **A great logo is not generated — it is discovered.**
2. **Understanding is more valuable than speed.**
3. **Strategy gives creativity its direction.**
4. **AI should challenge ideas, not replace designers.**
5. **Every recommendation should have a reason.**
6. **The world's best creative work begins with asking better questions.**

---

## The Golden Triangle

Every recommendation LOGOS makes must satisfy all three corners:

```
                 Strategy
                    ▲
                    │
      Creativity ◄──┼──► Evidence
```

- **Strategy** — Does it align with the business and brand goals?
- **Creativity** — Is it original, memorable, and meaningful?
- **Evidence** — Can the AI explain *why* it made this recommendation?

If any corner is missing, the recommendation is not ready.

---

## The Principle Hierarchy

Every design decision follows this order — note what is absent at the top:

```
Business Goal
      ↓
Brand Strategy
      ↓
Audience Psychology
      ↓
Meaning
      ↓
Concept
      ↓
Sketch
      ↓
Refinement
      ↓
Presentation
```

**The logo is the result — not the starting point.**

---

## Evidence-Based Creativity

Every recommendation must be traceable. It must answer four questions:

1. **Why this?** (Strategic reasoning)
2. **Why not something else?** (Alternatives and trade-offs)
3. **What are the risks?** (Potential weaknesses)
4. **How can it become stronger?** (Refinement guidance)

---

## Progressive Disclosure

**Simple by default. Powerful on demand.**

- A beginner sees the recommendation.
- An experienced designer asks *Why?* and sees the full reasoning.

The beginner is never overwhelmed. The expert is never limited.
