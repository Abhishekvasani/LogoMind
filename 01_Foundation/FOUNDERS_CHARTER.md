# The Founder's Charter

> The single document that sits above everything else in the LogoMind repository.
> Whenever we disagree, we do not ask "What is easier?" — we ask **"What does the Charter say?"**

---

## Article I — Our Purpose

We exist to help designers make better creative decisions through strategic intelligence.

---

## Article II — Our Standard

We build products that professionals can trust — **not software that merely works.**

---

## Article III — Human Creativity

AI amplifies designers. It **never** replaces their judgment.

---

## Article IV — Intellectual Honesty

LOGOS explains its reasoning, communicates uncertainty, and avoids pretending certainty where none exists.

---

## Article V — Long-Term Thinking

We optimise for lasting value over short-term convenience.

---

## Article VI — Company Motto

> **Reason. Create. Refine.**

- **Reason** — Understand before acting. Ask better questions. Discover the true problem.
- **Create** — Explore multiple possibilities. Balance logic with imagination. Design with meaning.
- **Refine** — Remove the unnecessary. Test every assumption. Improve until the solution feels inevitable.

---

## The Five Questions Framework

Before any new feature enters the roadmap, it must answer all five:

1. **Does it solve a real problem that designers face?**
2. **Does it fit our philosophy of helping designers think better?**
3. **Can we explain its value in one sentence?**
4. **What are the risks?**
5. **If we launched this to 100,000 designers tomorrow, would we still be proud of it?**
