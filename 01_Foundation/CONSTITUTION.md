# The LogoMind Constitution

## Preamble

LogoMind exists to improve the quality of design thinking. Every artifact, engine, and feature in this repository must serve that purpose. This Constitution is the highest governing document; all standards, knowledge objects, and software must align with it.

---

## Article I — Purpose

LogoMind's purpose is to **improve the quality of design decisions** made by identity designers. We measure success not by features shipped, but by judgment developed.

---

## Article II — The Designer Comes First

LogoMind is built for designers, not for AI users. The designer's judgment is sovereign. LogoMind recommends; the designer decides.

---

## Article III — Meaning Before Form

A logo is the visible consequence of an invisible idea. Understanding precedes design. Strategy precedes aesthetics. We never begin with visuals.

---

## Article IV — Explainability Over Mystery

Every recommendation LOGOS makes must be traceable to evidence, reasoning, and knowledge. We communicate uncertainty honestly. We never pretend certainty.

---

## Article V — Principles Stable, Practices Evolve

Core principles are timeless. Methods and tools may change. When a principle and a practice conflict, the principle wins.

---

## Article VI — Real Projects Are the Final Judge

All knowledge must ultimately be validated against real design practice. Theory without application is incomplete; application without reflection is wasted.

---

## Article VII — Excellence Is a Habit

Quality is not an event; it is a property of how we work. Every artifact is built to be world-class, or it is not built at all.

---

## The Seven LogoMind Virtues

Where the Articles define *beliefs*, the Virtues define *behaviour*:

| Virtue | Behaviour |
|--------|-----------|
| **Curiosity** | We ask better questions before reaching for answers. |
| **Clarity** | We make the complex understandable without dumbing it down. |
| **Discipline** | We resist feature creep and shortcut temptation. |
| **Humility** | We separate established knowledge from professional opinion. |
| **Craftsmanship** | We treat every artifact as if professionals will judge us by it. |
| **Courage** | We challenge weak ideas — including our own. |
| **Stewardship** | We build for the designers who will use this long after us. |

---

## The LogoMind Decision Filter (LDF)

Every feature, artifact, or change must pass:

1. Does it improve a designer's thinking?
2. Will it still make sense in 10 years?
3. Can we explain it in one sentence?
4. Does it respect the designer's creativity?
5. Would we proudly demo it to the world's best designers?

If any answer is *no*, we rethink it.

---

## Architecture Freeze

The **core LKOS** (this Constitution, the Philosophy, and LM-STD-001 through 006) is **frozen as v1.0**. Refinement requires one of:

- Repeated production friction across multiple LICs.
- A discovered contradiction.
- Strong, materially-improving evidence.

Until then, the foundation is treated as stable. We build *on* it, not *against* it.
