# Seed Registry

> Not every idea is implemented immediately. Seeds are future ideas captured here so they are never lost — and promoted to the roadmap only after repeated evidence demonstrates their value. **The AI should remember knowledge, not preferences.**

---

## How Seeds Work

```
Idea → Seed (captured here) → Draft → Knowledge Asset → Validated Knowledge → LOGOS Reasoning
```

A Seed is **not** a commitment. It is a memory. Seeds are reviewed periodically and promoted only when:
1. Repeated production friction shows they are needed.
2. Strong evidence justifies them.
3. They align with the Charter and pass the Five Questions Framework.

## Promotion Criteria

A Seed is promoted to the roadmap when it satisfies **all three**:

1. ✅ **Real problem** — It addresses friction encountered in actual production (not hypothetical).
2. ✅ **Repeated evidence** — The need has appeared more than once.
3. ✅ **Passes the Five Questions Framework** (see Founder's Charter).

Until then, seeds stay here — captured, not actioned.

---

## Review Cadence

The Seed Registry is reviewed:
- At the end of each Philosophy Series LIC (does production reveal a needed seed?).
- Monthly (do any seeds now have enough evidence to promote?).
- Before any new phase begins (do relevant seeds need promotion first?).
