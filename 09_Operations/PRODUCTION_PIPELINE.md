# LogoMind Production Pipeline

> Every RS-LIC must pass through this pipeline before publication.

```
Idea
  ↓
Blueprint          ← outline the LIC's purpose and structure
  ↓
Research           ← gather evidence, case studies, sources
  ↓
Knowledge Architecture  ← organise into the 12-section structure
  ↓
Draft              ← write each section to standard
  ↓
Editorial Review   ← 7-pass review (see below)
  ↓
Repository Review  ← verify standards conformance (LM-STD-001–006)
  ↓
Quality Review     ← score against LQRS (LM-STD-006)
  ↓
Publication        ← status → Approved / Reference Standard
  ↓
Continuous Improvement  ← validate against real projects; refine
```

---

## The 7-Pass Editorial Review

| Pass | Focus | Question |
|------|-------|----------|
| 1 | **Structural** | Are all sections present and in order? |
| 2 | **Conceptual** | Is the reasoning sound and complete? |
| 3 | **Editorial** | Is the language precise and clear? |
| 4 | **Educational** | Does it genuinely develop judgment? |
| 5 | **Evidence** | Are claims supported? Is evidence separated from interpretation? |
| 6 | **Designer Experience** | Would a professional designer find this valuable? |
| 7 | **Reference Standard** | Does it meet the bar for all future LICs? |

---

## Production Charter (6 Rules)

Every LIC must:

1. Teach principles before techniques.
2. Develop judgment before memory.
3. Explain reasoning before conclusions.
4. Separate evidence from interpretation.
5. Encourage reflection before certainty.
6. Leave the designer more capable than it found them.

---

## The 80/20 Build Rule (FD-003)

> 80% of effort goes to **production assets** (LICs, case studies, engines).
> 20% goes to **architecture and philosophy**.

If the ratio inverts, we are over-planning and under-producing.
