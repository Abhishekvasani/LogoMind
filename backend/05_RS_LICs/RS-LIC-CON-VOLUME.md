# Contest Dynamics — Starter Set

> *A contest is not a commission. The brief is thin, the feedback is scarce, and the client's taste arrives only through what they do — not what they say. Read behaviour, not words.*

---

## RS-LIC-CON-001 — Contest Formats

**Definition:** The format determines the risk geometry before a single pixel is drawn
**Guaranteed:** Prize is paid regardless of winner count — the holder is committed; stronger briefs usually
**Non-guaranteed:** Prize only if a winner is chosen — the holder can walk away; cap your total effort across the contest, never per entry
**Sealed:** Entries hidden from other designers — no leaderboard imitation possible; design for the brief alone
**Open (unsealed):** Everyone sees everyone — the holder sees trends across entries; revealed preference is observable (see CON-004)
**Blind first round:** Common in multi-round formats — early entries are judged without pile-on bias
**Best For:** Deciding effort allocation; interpreting WHY the room behaves as it does

---

## RS-LIC-CON-002 — The Contest Brief Reality

**Definition:** Contest briefs are structurally thin; treat them as signals, not specifications
**Typical state:** Short, semi-structured ("Company: …", "Colors in mind: …", "Anything to avoid: …"), often contradictory, written in minutes
**What the holder writes is what they ALREADY believe:** preferred/avoided colours and styles are the highest-signal lines — they are the brief's only pre-committed taste
**What is missing matters more:** no positioning, no audience, no meaning — the designer must supply strategy, because the holder cannot
**Price anchoring:** The prize level predicts the holder type (PSY-004 Pragmatist dominates at low tiers) — calibrate originality expectations: distinct WITHIN safe, not category-breaking
**Contradictions:** "modern but timeless", "simple but detailed" — decode via the Feedback Decoder; contradictions are unranked priorities, not errors
**Best For:** Brief decoding (Contest Reader); setting realistic originality bounds

## RS-LIC-CON-003 — Rating & Elimination Signals

**Definition:** Star ratings, eliminations, and comments are the holder's revealed preference — low information per event, high in aggregate
**Star ratings:** Often generous and sticky (an early 4-star survives on momentum); a DOWN-grade is a stronger signal than a low start
**Eliminations:** Clearest negative signal available — what got eliminated, and what it had in common, outweights what survived
**Comments:** The only channel with reasons — mine every adjective ("clean", "professional", "friendly" decode via PSY-009)
**No signals at all:** See CON-005 — do not mistake silence for rejection, nor for approval
**Aggregation rule:** One signal is noise; three aligned signals are a direction; a contradiction between signals means the holder is undecided, not that both are true
**Best For:** The Client Fit refine loop (translating contest_feedback into preference traits)

## RS-LIC-CON-004 — Reading the Room (Unsealed Contests)

**Definition:** Other designers' entries and the holder's reactions to them are free intelligence
**Watch what the holder rates UP on others' work:** Their revealed taste, stated more honestly than any brief line — patterns beat single picks
**Watch the leaderboard's common denominator:** If every top entry shares a property (colour, weight, symbol family), the room has found the holder's frequency — decide deliberately whether to join it or contrast it
**Imitation trap:** Copying the leaderboard's leader converges you into a tie with five others; the winning move is usually the same MEANING, sharper execution
**Contrast play:** In a room of one style, a disciplined opposite (same strategy, different execution) stands out — but only if the room's style is a cliché pile, not a holder preference
**Best For:** Direction selection when feedback is thin; Client Fit's contest-signal weighting

## RS-LIC-CON-005 — The No-Feedback Loop

**Definition:** Sealed formats, silent holders, and abandoned comment threads — designing without a compass
**The rule:** When behaviour gives nothing, the BRIEF is everything — its explicit likes/dislikes are the holder's only committed taste
**Budget discipline:** Decide total effort before entering (concept count, refinement rounds) and stop at budget — silence is not a reason to overspend
**Trend discipline:** Without holder signals, do NOT chase the platform's generic-contest aesthetic (gradient swoosh, swooshy smile) — the cliché lists still apply
**Expectation honesty:** Non-guaranteed + silent + crowded = lottery odds; treat entry value as portfolio + probability, and say so plainly
**Best For:** Preventing over-investment; keeping ungrounded contests strategic

---

## RS-LIC-CON-006 — Iteration Without Feedback

**Definition:** How to refine entries when the holder rates but never explains
**Iterate the signal, not the entry:** If 4 stars arrive with no comment, the direction is right — refine craft, do not redesign
**One variable per version:** Change symbol OR weight OR colour per iteration — never several, or the next signal is uninterpretable
**Version budget:** Two to three refined versions per concept; more versions of one idea rarely change a holder's mind
**Deletion beats burial:** When a concept is eliminated, do not resurrect it in a new colour — elimination is about MEANING, not finish
**Best For:** The refine loop's discipline; coaching designers against thrash

## Contest Signal Framework

| Signal observed | Likely meaning | The move |
|-------------------|----------------|----------|
| High rating, no comment | Direction right, craft ceiling not yet hit | Refine craft; do NOT redesign |
| Downgrade after a change | The last variable was wrong — identify WHICH | Revert the single newest change |
| Elimination cluster sharing a property | That property is a hard dislike | Remove the property everywhere; note it as a trait |
| Holder comments adjectives only | Taste-language — decode via Feedback Decoder | Translate to designable meaning; confirm in next version |
| Room converges on one style | Holder frequency found by others | Same meaning, sharper execution — or deliberate contrast if the style is cliché |
| Total silence | No committed signal beyond the brief | Design the brief's explicit likes; hold effort budget |

---

*LogoMind Principle: In a contest the client's words are scarce and their behaviour is abundant. Ratings, eliminations, and the room's convergence are data — aggregate before you act, change one variable at a time, and never mistake silence for instruction.*
