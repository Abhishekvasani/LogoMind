# Industry Intelligence — Starter Set (14 Categories)

> *Conventions are context, not commands. Know what every competitor does so the decision to follow or break it is a decision — and the clichés are never an option.*

Each entry covers the category's core signal (what its customers need to feel), its symbol/colour/typography conventions, the clichés that mark a mark as generic, and the genuine white-space opportunities.

---

## RS-LIC-IND-001 — Fintech & Finance

**Core Signal:** Trust and competence — "my money is safe here, with people who know what they're doing"
**Symbol Conventions:** Shield, coin, upward chart, pillar, handshake, lock
**Colour Conventions:** Navy/deep blue (trust), green (growth/money), gold accents (heritage banking)
**Typography Conventions:** Confident grotesque sans or transitional serif wordmarks; tabular-thinking, engineering-grade letterforms
**Clichés to Avoid:** Coin + chart combos, bitcoin-style circles with cut-outs, upward arrow through a letter, generic shield with a checkmark, skyline outlines
**Opportunities:** Craft-led trust (a wordmark that feels engraved/structural); unexpected warmth for challenger brands; abstraction of the category's core metaphor (growth as botanical geometry, security as keystone/arc architecture)
**LKG Relationships:** → RS-LIC-CL-001 Blue, → Trust, → Authority

## RS-LIC-IND-002 — Technology & SaaS

**Core Signal:** Clarity and momentum — "built by smart people, moving fast, simple to use"
**Symbol Conventions:** Abstract geometry, nodes/connections, letterform cut-outs, rounded-square app tiles
**Colour Conventions:** Blue-to-purple gradients, electric accents on near-black, single-hue minimalism
**Typography Conventions:** Geometric sans (circular o's, single-weight), lowercase wordmarks, tight tracking
**Clichés to Avoid:** Lightbulb, gear, circuit-board traces, generic "orbit swoosh", power button, binary rain, rocket ship
**Opportunities:** Extreme typographic confidence (the wordmark IS the brand); one precise custom letterform detail; negative-space function (the mark does what the product does)
**LKG Relationships:** → RS-LIC-TY-VOLUME, → Clarity, → Innovation

## RS-LIC-IND-003 — Healthcare & Medical

**Core Signal:** Care and competence — "you are safe, and we know what we're doing"
**Symbol Conventions:** Cross, pulse line, heart, hands cradling, leaf hybrids (wellness)
**Colour Conventions:** Blue (clinical calm), teal (modern care), green (wellness), white space
**Typography Conventions:** Humanist sans with open apertures (approachable, legible at every size)
**Clichés to Avoid:** Caduceus misuse, generic pulse-in-a-circle, tooth in a shield (dental), cross + heart mashups, smiling-family silhouettes
**Opportunities:** Warmth without losing authority (soft geometry, warm neutrals); the human gesture abstracted; condition-specific precision (a mark that references the specialty's craft, not generic care)
**LKG Relationships:** → RS-LIC-CL-010 Teal, → Care, → Clarity

## RS-LIC-IND-004 — Food & Beverage

**Core Signal:** Appetite and identity — "this tastes like something, and we stand for something"
**Symbol Conventions:** The ingredient itself, chef's elements, fork/knife abstraction, farm references
**Colour Conventions:** Red/orange (appetite), earth tones (craft/artisanal), cream/charcoal (premium dining)
**Typography Conventions:** Everything from hand-cut display serif (premium) to chunky retro sans (fast-casual) — script for artisanal
**Clichés to Avoid:** Chef hat, generic fork-and-spoon circle, plate-and-steam, "est." badges without heritage, cartoon mascot eating, crossed utensils
**Opportunities:** Ingredient-as-geometry (the food rendered as precise form); cultural specificity done with respect (actual craft reference, not stereotype); menu-typography systems that scale
**LKG Relationships:** → RS-LIC-CL-003 Red, → Warmth, → Craft

## RS-LIC-IND-006 — Fashion & Apparel

**Colour Conventions:** Black/white (editorial confidence), single accent, tonal neutrals

## Industry Intelligence Framework

| Category Situation | Rule |
|-------------------|------|
| Convention carries trust (banking, legal, healthcare) | Honour the convention's SIGNAL; differentiate through craft — treatment, letterforms, composition — not by swapping the symbol for a novelty |
| Convention is a sea of sameness (fintech blue, eco green) | Keep the signal, break the default execution: same trust delivered by an unexpected form |
| No strong convention (new/niche category) | Borrow the nearest credible category's signal — and make the borrow a stated strategy |
| Clichés list | Treat as hard constraints. Never generate. If the client requests one, refresh the underlying meaning — never replicate the familiar form |
| Opportunity entry | Only claim with a traceable line to Brand DNA. An opportunity without brand support is decoration |
| Unknown industry | Apply the closest analogue's rules and say which analogue was used |
