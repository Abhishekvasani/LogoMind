# LM-STD-005 — Knowledge Layering

> Knowledge is **layered, not linear.** One artifact serves three audiences — beginner, professional, and expert — through structured depth, not separate documents.

---

## 1. Purpose

A single RS-LIC must serve a designer on their first day and a creative director with twenty years of experience. The solution is not separate documents — it is **layered depth** within one source of truth.

---

## 2. The Five Knowledge Layers

| Layer | Name | Question Answered | Contents |
|-------|------|-------------------|----------|
| **A** | Foundational | "What is this?" | Definitions, core principles, the essential idea in 60 seconds. |
| **B** | Professional Understanding | "How do professionals use this?" | Application, decision frameworks, common practice, heuristics. |
| **C** | Applied | "How do I execute this?" | Worked examples, decision studio scenarios, step-by-step methods. |
| **D** | Evidence & Validation | "What supports this?" | Case studies, research, citations, confidence assessment. |
| **E** | Reflective Growth | "How do I deepen my judgment?" | Reflection questions, edge cases, mastery notes, trade-offs. |

---

## 3. Layer Relationships

```
Layer A  Foundational        ← everyone reads this
   ↓
Layer B  Professional        ← professionals work here
   ↓
Layer C  Applied             ← execution and practice
   ↓
Layer D  Evidence            ← what supports the above
   ↓
Layer E  Reflective Growth   ← mastery and reflection
```

Knowledge flows downward: a principle (Layer A) is explained professionally (B), applied (C), validated (D), and reflected upon (E).

---

## 4. The Layer Integrity Rule

**Knowledge never moves downward.** A Layer A definition is never redefined by Layer B content. Layer B explains and applies; it does not overwrite. This preserves a single source of truth.

---

## 5. Layer Transition Questions

Each layer answers a distinct question. When content blurs the boundary, ask:

- *Is this defining the concept (A) or applying it (B/C)?*
- *Is this supporting a claim (D) or making one (A/B)?*
- *Is this inviting reflection (E) or delivering content (A–D)?*

---

## 7. Progressive Disclosure in Practice

- A **beginner** reads Layer A and understands the concept.
- A **professional** reads Layers A–C and can apply it.
- An **expert** reads all layers, including trade-offs and open questions.

LOGOS determines which layer to surface based on the designer's context (see AI Design Principle 7 — Reasoning Depth Is Adaptive).

---

## Relationship to Other Standards

- **LM-STD-001** (Learning Contract) declares *what* is taught at each layer.
- **LM-STD-003** (Confidence) is often higher in Layer A and lower in Layers C–E.
- **LM-STD-006** (Quality Review) verifies each layer fulfils its purpose without leaking into another.
