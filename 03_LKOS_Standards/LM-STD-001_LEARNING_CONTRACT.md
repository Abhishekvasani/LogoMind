# LM-STD-001 — Learning Contract

> A Learning Contract is a **formal agreement** between the knowledge artifact and the designer who studies it — not an introduction.

---

## 1. Purpose

Every RS-LIC (Reference Standard Intelligence Card) must declare what it teaches, to whom, and what the designer will be able to do after studying it. Without a Learning Contract, content is merely information.

---

## 2. Required Structure

Every Learning Contract contains:

| Section | Question Answered |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Purpose** | Why does this knowledge exist? |
| **Professional Relevance** | Why should a designer care? |
| **Intended Audience** | Who is this for? (Beginner / Professional / Expert) |
| **Prerequisites** | What must the designer already understand? |
| **Learning Outcomes** | What will the designer be able to *do* after studying this? |
| **Scope** | What is included? What is explicitly excluded? |
| **Recommended Study Path** | How should the designer approach this material? |
| **Mastery Expectations** | What distinguishes understanding from mastery? |

---

## 3. Learning Outcomes Are Capability-Based

Outcomes must describe **capabilities**, not topics covered.

- ❌ "Understand the concept of meaning."
- ✅ "Determine whether a design decision is driven by meaning or by aesthetics, and justify the choice."

---

## 4. Learning Integrity Rule

A Learning Contract may not promise what the content does not deliver. If the body of the LIC cannot fulfil an outcome, the outcome is removed or the content is strengthened.

---

## 5. Completion Criteria

The designer has "completed" a LIC when they can:

1. State the core principle in their own words.
2. Apply the decision framework to a novel brief.
3. Recognise and name the common mistakes.
4. Defend a design choice using the LIC's reasoning.

---

## Relationship to Other Standards

- **LM-STD-002** (Statement Taxonomy) defines the *types* of statements used to fulfil the contract.
- **LM-STD-005** (Knowledge Layering) defines the *depth* at which the contract is delivered.
- **LM-STD-006** (Quality Review) verifies the contract is actually met.
